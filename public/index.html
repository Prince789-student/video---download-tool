Frequently Asked Questions

How do I download an Instagram Reel or Post?
Open Instagram, tap the Share icon on any public Reel or video, click "Copy Link", and paste it into the search box above. Click Analyze Media and select your preferred quality.
Why do high-resolution 1080p / 4K videos have audio attached?
Platforms like YouTube serve high-definition video streams and audio streams separately. Our server uses built-in FFmpeg muxing to seamlessly fuse high-definition video with pristine audio into a single MP4 file before saving to your device.
Can I download private videos or age-restricted content?
Only public videos accessible without personal account logins can be processed. Private accounts or age-gated media require authentication and cannot be parsed.
